Smart India Hackathon – 2025

Gamified Learning Platform for Rural Education

Role: Team Leader – Smart India Hackathon 2025

Developed a gamified multilingual learning platform aimed at improving education accessibility for rural students. The platform supports Odia voice-based learning, AI assistance, and localized content to enable inclusive and engaging education.

  • ✦Gamified learning with avatars, rewards, pets, outfits, and leaderboards
  • ✦Odia voice input and audio output for non-English learners
  • ✦Localized content based on Odisha cities and culture
  • ✦AI chatbot and image-based learning (Google Lens–like scanner)
  • ✦Teacher dashboard to track progress and identify students needing attention
  • ✦Assignments, overdue tracking, virtual labs, and mini-games
